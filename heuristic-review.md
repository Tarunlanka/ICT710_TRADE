# Heuristic Review

## Review scope
Expert review of the proposed mobile trading flows, focusing on clarity, consistency, error prevention, feedback, and user control.

## Potential issues and design responses
- **Visibility of system status:** Provide clear progress in the five-step wizard and a receipt after submission.
- **Match between system and real-world language:** Use Add Money, Withdraw, From, and To rather than ambiguous directional terms.
- **User control and freedom:** Allow back/edit before confirmation; provide pause/cancel controls for automation.
- **Consistency and standards:** Keep Buy/Sell actions and confirmation patterns in predictable locations.
- **Error prevention:** Validate fields and display fees and totals before final confirmation.
- **Recognition rather than recall:** Summarise account direction and automation rules on review screens.
- **Help and documentation:** Provide contextual explanations at complex steps.

## Limitations
This is a proposed expert-review summary. Do not assign severity scores or claim independent reviewers completed the inspection unless those activities and scores are documented.
