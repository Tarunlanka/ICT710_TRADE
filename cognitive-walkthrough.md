# Cognitive Walkthrough Notes

## Scope
These notes describe an expert inspection of proposed CLEAR TRADE flows. They are not a record of a completed participant test.

## Walkthrough questions
For each step, ask:
1. Will the user try to achieve the right outcome?
2. Is the correct action visible?
3. Will the user associate the action with the intended outcome?
4. After acting, will feedback make progress clear?

## Priority scenario: fund transfer
- Is Add Money versus Withdraw clear?
- Are source and destination accounts named?
- Is transfer direction repeated before confirmation?
- Are amount, fees, and timing visible before submission?
- Does the receipt confirm what happened?

## Priority scenario: automated trading
- Can users identify the asset and intended direction?
- Are trigger and action explained in understandable terms?
- Are limits and expiry settings discoverable?
- Does the review summary accurately describe the rule?
- Is activation explicit, and can the rule later be paused or cancelled?

## Finding status
This is an inspection framework and documented design rationale. Do not report it as empirical user-test evidence. Record actual observations, dates, and inspectors if this walkthrough is carried out formally.
