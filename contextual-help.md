# Contextual Help

## Purpose
Provide help at the point of difficulty, particularly during fund transfer and automated-trading setup.

## Proposed behaviour
- Show short explanations beside unfamiliar fields.
- Offer examples of valid input formats.
- Provide “Why do we need this?” explanations for consequential settings.
- Keep help available without forcing users to leave the current task.
- Offer a clear route to broader support when contextual guidance is insufficient.

## Content guidelines
Use plain language, concise sentences, and specific action-oriented instructions. Avoid unexplained financial jargon and do not make promises about returns or outcomes.

## Evaluation
Check whether users find help when needed, understand it, and can resume the task. Track help use alongside task completion and error counts.
